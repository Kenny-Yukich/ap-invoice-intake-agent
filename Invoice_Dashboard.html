Green bars feed the pipeline. The red bar is deliberately never used.

The load-bearing finding: on zero-tax invoices, subtotal and total are identical numbers — the model hedges between two equally valid readings and confidence on InvoiceTotal collapses to ~53%, while individual line items extract at 93–96%. So the pipeline computes its own total from line items and gates every money-relevant field at a 0.80 floor (rule 6). The system is built to distrust the AI exactly where the AI is weak.

Every terminal decision writes a row: who, when, what, and the human's own words. Rows are only ever created — never edited. That's what makes it an audit trail rather than a status field.
